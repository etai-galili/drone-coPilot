DJI AVATA 2 — FULL SPECIFICATIONS REFERENCE

AIRCRAFT
- Takeoff weight: approximately 377 g.
- Dimensions: 185 x 212 x 64 mm (L x W x H).
- Max ascent speed: 6 m/s (Normal), 9 m/s (Sport).
- Max descent speed: 6 m/s (Normal), 9 m/s (Sport).
- Max horizontal speed: 8 m/s (Normal), 16 m/s (Sport), 27 m/s (Manual).
- Max takeoff altitude (service ceiling): 5000 m.
- Max flight time: approximately 23 minutes (forward flight at 21.6 km/h, windless, sea level).
- Max hovering time: approximately 21 minutes (windless, sea level).
- Max wind resistance: Level 5 (approximately 10.7 m/s).
- Operating temperature: -10 C to 40 C.
- Internal storage: 46 GB; microSD card supported.
- Positioning: GNSS + downward binocular vision + infrared sensing system.

CAMERA
- Sensor: 1/1.3-inch CMOS.
- Effective pixels: 12 MP.
- FOV: 155 degrees.
- Aperture: f/2.8 (fixed).
- ISO: 100-25600.
- Video: up to 4K/60fps; 2.7K up to 120fps; 1080p up to 120fps.
- Photo: 12 MP (JPEG).
- Stabilization: single-axis mechanical gimbal + EIS (RockSteady, HorizonSteady).

VIDEO TRANSMISSION (DJI O4)
- System: DJI O4.
- Live view to Goggles 3: 1080p at up to 100 fps.
- Latency: approximately 24 ms.
- Max bitrate: 60 Mbps.
- Max range (unobstructed, interference-free): 13 km (FCC) / 10 km (CE / SRRC / MIC).
- Frequency bands: 2.4 GHz, 5.1 GHz, 5.8 GHz (region dependent).

INTELLIGENT FLIGHT BATTERY
- Capacity: 2150 mAh.
- Nominal voltage: 14.76 V.
- Energy: 31.7 Wh.
- Chemistry: LiPo 4S.
- Weight: approximately 145 g.

SYSTEM COMPONENTS
- Goggles: DJI Goggles 3.
- Controller: DJI RC Motion 3 (motion controller); DJI FPV Remote Controller 3 supported for Manual/acro flight.
- Charging: DJI USB-C charger; Two-Way Charging Hub in Fly More Combo.

FLIGHT MODES
- Normal (N): GNSS + vision positioning, obstacle braking, beginner-safe.
- Sport (S): higher speed, reduced obstacle protection.
- Manual (M): full FPV acro, no stabilization or obstacle braking (expert only).

NOTES ON ACCURACY
- All figures above reflect DJI Avata 2 (not the original Avata). The original DJI Avata used the O3+ transmission system and a ~18-minute flight time; the Avata 2 uses DJI O4 and ~23-minute flight time. Do not mix the two models' specifications.
