DJI AVATA 2 — OPERATOR INSTRUCTIONS AND CHECKLISTS

PRE-FLIGHT CHECKLIST (STANDARD)
1. Inspect the airframe, ducts, and all four propellers for cracks, chips, or debris.
2. Confirm propellers are seated and the ducts are intact.
3. Verify the Intelligent Flight Battery is fully charged and clicked in firmly.
4. Check the goggles battery charge and that the headband/lens are clean.
5. Power on the goggles, then the aircraft; confirm they link.
6. Confirm microSD card is inserted (or sufficient internal storage) and formatted if needed.
7. Verify firmware on aircraft, goggles, and controller is up to date and matched.
8. Acquire GNSS lock (outdoors) or confirm vision positioning (indoors) before takeoff.
9. Set Home Point and RTH altitude above the tallest nearby obstacle.
10. Select the correct flight mode for the environment (Normal indoors, Sport open areas).
11. Confirm the flight area is clear of people, animals, and obstacles.
12. Confirm wind is within Level 5 (about 10.7 m/s) and weather is dry.

INDOOR / CONFINED-SPACE OPERATION
- Use Normal Mode for positioning and obstacle braking.
- Keep ducts as the primary contact protection; the Avata 2 is designed to tolerate light brushes with obstacles, but avoid deliberate contact.
- Ensure adequate lighting for the downward vision sensors.
- Maintain a safe distance from people; the ducts reduce but do not eliminate injury risk.

OUTDOOR OPERATION
- Acquire a strong GNSS lock (sufficient satellites) before takeoff.
- Verify airspace is legal for flight and observe altitude limits.
- Check wind at altitude, not just at ground level.
- Use a visual observer for FPV flight as required by local rules.

IN-FLIGHT DISCIPLINE
- Monitor battery percentage and return with reserve (land by ~20-25%).
- Watch the goggles for low-signal, low-battery, and obstacle warnings.
- Keep the aircraft within video transmission range and, where required, visual line of sight.
- If signal degrades, reduce distance and turn the aircraft to face the operator (antennas point forward/down).

POST-FLIGHT CHECKLIST
1. Land, disarm, and power down the aircraft, then the goggles and controller.
2. Allow the battery and motors to cool before charging or storing.
3. Inspect propellers and ducts for damage incurred in flight.
4. Offload footage and back up critical recordings.
5. Log flight time, battery cycles, and any anomalies.

BATTERY HANDLING RULES
- Charge only with DJI chargers / the Two-Way Charging Hub.
- Do not charge a hot battery; let it cool first.
- For storage longer than a few days, keep batteries at about 40-65% charge.
- Never fly a swollen, punctured, or damaged battery.

SAFETY PRIORITIES
- Operator and bystander safety come before the mission and before the aircraft.
- In any doubt about airworthiness, do not fly.
- For emergencies, act first (LAND IMMEDIATELY or ACTIVATE RTH NOW), then diagnose.
