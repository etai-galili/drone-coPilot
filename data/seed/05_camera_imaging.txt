DJI AVATA 2 — CAMERA, GIMBAL, AND IMAGING SYSTEM

CAMERA / SENSOR (VERIFIED SPECS)
- Sensor: 1/1.3-inch CMOS.
- Effective resolution: 12 MP.
- Field of view: 155 degrees (ultra-wide).
- Aperture: f/2.8 (fixed).
- ISO range: 100-25600 (Auto and Manual).
- Lens: fixed-focus wide-angle; no optical zoom.

STABILIZATION
- Single-axis mechanical gimbal (tilt) combined with electronic image stabilization.
- RockSteady electronic stabilization smooths footage during aggressive FPV maneuvers.
- HorizonSteady keeps the horizon level even when the aircraft rolls.
- The gimbal tilt range allows aiming the camera up and down for diving and climbing shots.

VIDEO MODES (TYPICAL)
- 4K: up to 60 fps (also 50/48/30/25/24).
- 2.7K: up to 120 fps for slow motion.
- 1080p: up to 120 fps.
- Color profiles: Standard and a flat profile for grading.
- Codec/container: H.264 / H.265 in MP4.

PHOTO MODE
- 12 MP still images.
- Single-shot stills in JPEG.

LIVE VIEW AND VIDEO TRANSMISSION (DJI O4, VERIFIED)
- Transmission system: DJI O4.
- Live feed to DJI Goggles 3: 1080p at up to 100 fps.
- Latency: approximately 24 ms.
- Maximum bitrate: 60 Mbps.
- Maximum transmission range (unobstructed, interference-free): 13 km (FCC) / 10 km (CE / SRRC / MIC).
- Operating frequency bands: 2.4 GHz, 5.1 GHz, and 5.8 GHz (region dependent).

STORAGE
- Internal storage: 46 GB on board.
- External: microSD card supported for additional capacity.
- Footage can be offloaded via USB-C.

IMAGING OPERATION NOTES
- Set resolution and frame rate in the goggles menu before flight to match the intended edit.
- Higher frame rates and resolutions consume storage faster; confirm free space pre-flight.
- The wide FOV exaggerates speed and is ideal for immersive FPV; mind the lens for sun flare on bright days.
- Keep the lens and gimbal guard clean; remove the gimbal protector before powering on for flight.

CAMERA TROUBLESHOOTING
- Shaky footage: confirm RockSteady/HorizonSteady is enabled and propellers are balanced/undamaged.
- Recording stops unexpectedly: check storage space, card health (use a recommended high-speed card), and overheating.
- Tilted horizon: recalibrate the gimbal/IMU on a level surface.
- No live image in goggles: re-check the aircraft-goggles link and that the gimbal protector is removed.
