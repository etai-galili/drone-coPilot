DJI AVATA 2 — KNOWN FAULTS AND TROUBLESHOOTING

This section lists common, recognized faults for the Avata 2 system and their standard resolutions. For any safety-critical doubt, do not fly and check the official manual.

POWER AND BATTERY
- Aircraft will not power on: battery may be in deep-sleep storage state — connect to a charger briefly, then retry. Confirm the battery is seated and the bay latch is fully engaged.
- Battery not recognized / connection fault: clean the battery and bay contacts; check for moisture or corrosion.
- Very short flight time: cold battery, aged cells (high cycle count), or aggressive flying. Warm the battery, check cycle count, and retire degraded packs.
- Battery overheats or swells: retire the battery immediately and recycle it safely. Do not fly or charge it.

POSITIONING AND STABILITY
- Drifts in hover: weak GNSS lock or poor lighting for downward vision sensors. Move to open sky / improve lighting; recalibrate IMU and compass on a level surface.
- Alternating Red/Yellow status LED (IMU/sensor error): do not fly; recalibrate the IMU on a flat, level surface and restart.
- Tilted horizon in flight/footage: recalibrate gimbal and IMU; confirm HorizonSteady setting.
- Toilet-bowl effect / circling: compass interference or bad calibration — move away from metal/magnetic sources and recalibrate.

VIDEO TRANSMISSION AND GOGGLES
- No live image in goggles: confirm aircraft-goggles link, re-pair if needed, and remove the gimbal protector.
- Laggy or breaking-up feed: interference or range. Reduce distance, point the aircraft's antennas (front/underside) toward the operator, change channel/frequency, and avoid Wi-Fi-dense areas.
- Goggles will not link: power-cycle both, verify matched firmware, and re-run the linking procedure.
- Blurry goggles display: adjust the diopter/focus and lens spacing; clean the lenses.

CAMERA AND RECORDING
- Recording stops or will not start: storage full, slow/incompatible card, or overheating. Free space, use a recommended high-speed microSD, and let the unit cool.
- Shaky footage: enable RockSteady/HorizonSteady; inspect and replace damaged/imbalanced propellers.

MECHANICAL
- Vibration and unstable flight: damaged or unbalanced propellers — replace in matched sets.
- Motor grinding/wobble: foreign object or worn bearing; service or replace the motor; do not fly.
- Cracked duct: replace before flying.

LINK, FIRMWARE, AND SYSTEM
- Repeated disconnects or odd behavior after an update: ensure aircraft, goggles, and controller firmware are all updated and matched; restart all components.
- Geofencing blocks takeoff: the aircraft is in a restricted/authorization zone — verify location and complete required authorization, or relocate.

RETURN-TO-HOME ISSUES
- RTH flies to the wrong spot: Home Point was not refreshed at the latest takeoff location, or GNSS was weak at takeoff. Always confirm Home Point is updated before flight.
- RTH ascends/descends unexpectedly: RTH altitude is set too low/high — set it above the tallest nearby obstacle.

WHEN IN DOUBT
- If a fault is safety-critical (battery damage, motor failure, IMU error, structural duct damage), do not fly.
- Recalibrate, update firmware, and replace worn parts before returning to service.
