DJI AVATA 2 — EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

For every emergency: act first, then diagnose. Prioritize bystander and operator safety over the aircraft.

RETURN-TO-HOME (RTH)
- Manual RTH: press and hold the RTH button on the RC Motion 3. The aircraft ascends to the set RTH altitude, flies back to the Home Point, and lands.
- Smart RTH: triggered automatically on signal loss or critical low battery.
- Cancel RTH if you need to take manual control to avoid an obstacle (RTH has limited obstacle avoidance).
- Ensure RTH altitude is set above the tallest obstacle on the return path before every flight.

SIGNAL LOSS (LOST CONNECTION)
- The aircraft executes its Lost-Connection behavior (RTH by default) after the link is lost.
- Pilot action: stop moving the controller, reduce distance toward the aircraft, point the operator side toward the aircraft, and wait for the link to recover. The feed often reconnects as range/interference improves.

LOW AND CRITICAL BATTERY
- Low Battery Warning: begin returning immediately and land with reserve.
- Critical Low Battery: the aircraft auto-lands where it is if it cannot reach Home. Steer toward a safe landing area while it descends if you still have control.

INVERTED / CRASHED ON TOP (TURTLE MODE)
- If the aircraft lands upside down with propellers spinning-capable, use Turtle Mode from the goggles to flip it upright.
- Use only in clear, open areas and keep hands, people, and objects away from the propellers during the maneuver.

OBSTACLE / IMMINENT COLLISION
- Press the lock button to brake and hover instantly (Normal Mode).
- In Sport/Manual there is reduced or no obstacle protection — manually steer clear.

FLYAWAY / UNCONTROLLED FLIGHT
- Trigger RTH immediately.
- If RTH fails, switch flight mode and attempt manual recovery toward a safe area.
- As a last resort to prevent harm to people, command an emergency descent/landing.

EMERGENCY MOTOR STOP
- Emergency stop cuts power to the motors mid-air and will cause the aircraft to fall. Use only to prevent greater harm (e.g., the aircraft is heading toward a person), never as a routine landing method.

FIRE / DAMAGED BATTERY
- If a battery smokes, swells, or ignites, move it to a non-flammable area if safe to do so and keep clear; LiPo fires are intense. Do not attempt to charge or reuse it.

POST-EMERGENCY
- After any hard landing or crash, power down and inspect propellers, ducts, motors, gimbal, and battery before any further flight.
- Recalibrate IMU/compass and verify all systems before returning to service.

QUICK EMERGENCY REFERENCE
- Lost link: hold position, reduce distance — RTH engages automatically.
- Low battery: RETURN NOW; land with reserve.
- Critical battery: aircraft auto-lands; steer to safety.
- Obstacle: press lock to brake (Normal Mode).
- Inverted: Turtle Mode in clear areas only.
- Heading toward people: emergency motor stop as last resort.
